DAILY WORKFLOW

Find, save, draft, review, close

Five steps. A human approves every draft. Nothing is ever sent to LinkedIn automatically.

STEP 1

Find

Open a LinkedIn profile, post, company page, or conversation. The extension side panel detects the page type and reads only the visible text.

Where: extension side panel
→
STEP 2

Save

Save the person as a prospect with company, title, industry, and a relevance score with a written reason. The prospect lands in stage Target.

Stage: Target, weight 2
→
STEP 3

Draft

Ask for a connection message, comment, or follow-up. The strategist builds the prompt from real database evidence only and the model drafts it.

AI: free models only, 12 model chain
→
STEP 4

Review

The draft sits in the approval queue as pending. You read it, then approve or reject it. Approving writes an audit log row.

Gate: kill switch blocks approval
→
STEP 5

Close

Move the prospect through the pipeline as the relationship grows. Record interactions and notes, and set a follow-up date.

End: Won, weight 1. Lost, weight 0.
Loop: a rejected draft returns to Draft with feedback. An approved draft is copied by hand. Nothing is ever sent to LinkedIn automatically.
RUNS ON
Localhost, port 3000. Node 20, Express 5.
STORED IN

PROTECTED BY
Approval queue, kill switch, audit log.
